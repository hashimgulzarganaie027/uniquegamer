📘 Essential Pre-Setup Guide

Before You Open the App

After downloading and installing Unique Tool, please configure Shizuku first if your device runs Android 11 or higher. If you do not know how to set up and start Shizuku using Wireless Debugging, you can easily watch a step-by-step tutorial on YouTube.

▶ Watch Shizuku Setup Tutorial on YouTube
Which Permission Should You Choose?
Android 1 to 10
Use SAF Permission

If your phone runs on Android 1 through Android 10, simply grant the SAF (Storage Access Framework) permission when prompted inside the app.

Android 11 to 17
Use Shizuku Only

If your phone runs on Android 11 through Android 17, you must start Shizuku and grant Shizuku Permission inside the app.

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNING FOR ANDROID 11–17 USERS

Please pay close attention: If your device is on Android 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, or 17, you MUST select and grant the Shizuku Permission only. Do not use standard/SAF permission on Android 11–17, otherwise the system will block access to game folders and you will face errors!

How to Use Unique Tool
1

How to Download & Install

Go to the Download tab above and tap the Download APK button. Once downloaded, install the APK on your device, launch the app, and make sure your internet connection is turned on to verify server access.

2

How to Apply Hologram

Open the main control panel and grant the required permission based on your Android version (SAF for Android 1–10, or Shizuku for Android 11–17). Expand either the Free Fire Hologram or Free Fire Max Hologram section, turn ON the apply switch, and wait for the progress bar to reach 100%. Once completed, use the bottom quick-launch buttons to open your game.

3

How to Remove Hologram

Whenever you want to remove the Hologram and restore your clean directory, simply open the app and toggle ON the Remove Hologram switch for your game (Free Fire or Free Fire Max). Our built-in cleaner engine will scan the target folder, delete all applied Hologram files smoothly from 0% to 100%, and confirm when the cleanup is complete.
